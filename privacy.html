Cosmic Dream Bog Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cosmic Dream Bog collects, uses, stores, and protects information associated with the Cosmic Dream Bog website, Discord community, Hunt Bot, games, rewards, and related services.

1. Information We May Collect

When you participate in Cosmic Dream Bog, we may collect limited information necessary to operate the experience.

This may include:

  • Your Discord user ID
  • Your Discord display name or username
  • Your game stage and progression
  • Hunt, encounter, or reward progress
  • Roles assigned through the Hunt Bot
  • A public blockchain wallet address you voluntarily submit
  • Reward eligibility and delivery status

2. Wallet Security

Cosmic Dream Bog will never request or store your wallet seed phrase, recovery phrase, private key, wallet password, or Discord password.

Only a public wallet address may be requested when needed to deliver a digital reward.

Never provide private wallet credentials to the Hunt Bot, moderators, administrators, or anyone claiming to represent Cosmic Dream Bog.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Operate the Cosmic Dream Bog Hunt Bot
  • Track game progression
  • Assign Discord progression roles
  • Unlock appropriate game channels
  • Prevent duplicate or abusive reward claims
  • Record eligibility for digital rewards
  • Deliver earned rewards to submitted public wallet addresses
  • Troubleshoot or improve the game experience
  • Protect the community from fraud, abuse, or exploitation

4. Reward Records

Cosmic Dream Bog may maintain records connecting a Discord participant with a submitted public wallet address and earned reward.

These records may be used to prepare periodic reward distributions, including Cosmic Dream Bog Alliance Dispatches.

5. Duplicate Claim Prevention

Where a reward is limited to one per participant, information may be reviewed to identify duplicate Discord accounts, repeated claims, or repeated use of the same wallet address.

These checks are intended to maintain fair participation and protect legitimate community members.

6. Data Sharing

Cosmic Dream Bog does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed through service providers necessary to operate the project, such as Discord, website hosting providers, database or server hosting providers, blockchain networks, and wallet infrastructure.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect the project or its users, investigate fraud or abuse, or enforce community rules.

7. Blockchain Information

Public blockchain transactions are generally visible permanently on the applicable blockchain.

Cosmic Dream Bog cannot remove or alter information that has already been recorded on a public blockchain.

8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the game, maintain reward records, prevent abuse, satisfy legitimate administrative needs, or comply with applicable requirements.

Records that are no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized where reasonably practical.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You may contact Cosmic Dream Bog to request deletion of information associated with your participation.

Some records may need to be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or completed blockchain transactions.

Requests may be sent to: cosmicdreambog@hotmail.com

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information under Cosmic Dream Bog's control. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Participants should protect their Discord accounts and blockchain wallets with strong passwords, multi-factor authentication where available, and appropriate wallet security practices.

11. Third-Party Services

Cosmic Dream Bog relies on third-party platforms and services, which may include Discord, blockchain networks, hosting providers, wallet software, and other technical infrastructure.

Those services have their own privacy practices and policies.

12. Children's Privacy

Cosmic Dream Bog is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not permitted to use the applicable services under their local laws or the requirements of third-party platforms such as Discord.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Cosmic Dream Bog evolves. The current version will be posted on this page with its latest revision date.

14. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to:
