TRANSMISSION 01

Not another drop. A call to enter the Bog.

Most launches begin by showing people what they can buy. Cosmic Dream Bog begins differently. Participants are invited into a mystery. They enter because someone they trust gave them the signal. They discover what is happening by being present, paying attention and moving through the story together.

Your role in the Alliance is not to become part of the Cosmic Dream Bog team. You remain the leader of your own project and your own community. The Alliance simply creates the bridge between those communities and the Bog.

TRANSMISSION 02

The Bog is already waiting.

Cosmic Dream Bog exists around a specific location inside the Otherside ecosystem: a Bog environment shaped by Cosmic Dream sediment. The setting is strange, unstable and dreamlike.

The Obelisk

Something has shattered.

At the center of the mystery is an ancient obelisk that has been deliberately broken. Its fragments have scattered throughout the Bog, and the reason for its destruction becomes one of the central questions of the story.

The Shards

Discovery has consequences.

Shards, clues and fragments become part of the investigation. Some will be discovered through the Discord experience. Others will require participants to pay attention to what the Bog is revealing.

The Discord

The live front door.

Discord is where the active campaign unfolds: gated chapters, clues, community discoveries, Shard Hunt activity and story progression.

The Tone

Strange. Eerie. Playful.

The project sits inside interactive horror rather than traditional horror. The Bog should feel mysterious and unsettling without losing the weird, communal energy that makes discovery fun.

TRANSMISSION 03

Four movements. One shared story.

Participants do not receive the entire story at once. The world changes chapter by chapter.

01

Answer the Call

Selected communities receive the first signal. Those who choose to answer enter the Bog and begin their path.

02

The Shattering

Participants discover that the obelisk has been destroyed. New spaces open. The Shard Hunt begins, and the first pieces of the mystery appear.

03

The Investigation

Clues, puzzles, fragments and strange discoveries begin connecting the communities to the same question: what happened here, and why?

04

The Rally

Separate communities become one expedition. The investigation moves toward restoration of the obelisk, confrontation with the force behind its destruction and the final path.

TRANSMISSION 04

There is someone else in the Bog.

The destruction was not an accident. Behind the Shattering is a figure known as the Dream Herald. His interest in the obelisk and its power is part of a larger purpose that participants will slowly uncover.

The Alliance does not need to explain every secret to participants. In fact, it should not. The story works because communities discover the truth together rather than receiving a roadmap filled with spoilers.

TRANSMISSION 05

The path is deliberately simple.

Rule One

One wallet. One path.

Each participant follows the journey through a single wallet. The system is designed around participation, not stacking.

Rule Two

Progress at your own pace.

The campaign is asynchronous. Someone arriving later can still move through earlier chapters rather than being permanently locked out because they missed a specific day.

Rule Three

Three story mints.

Three story NFTs act as progression markers through the journey. Each one represents movement deeper into the campaign and helps determine access to what comes next.

Rule Four

The final reward is earned.

Completing the journey creates one final mint opportunity for that qualifying wallet.

TRANSMISSION 06

What we are asking from the Alliance.

The contribution is intentionally light. This is not a paid promotional campaign and it is not a request to repeatedly market Cosmic Dream Bog.

Your role is to become the signal.

1
Receive the private call.

Alliance partners receive the initial material and context before their community enters the story.

2
Select the people you trust.

Share the call with holders or community members you believe would genuinely enjoy solving, discovering and participating.

3
Point them toward Discord.

Discord becomes their live entrance into the campaign and the place where the active story progresses.

4
Join key moments when it fits.

Alliance partners are welcome to appear during important chapter moments, but there is no expectation that you constantly promote or manage the experience.

Shard Hunt

Gated Chapters

The story opens progressively.

Story progression and wallet ownership eventually determine access to deeper areas of the campaign.

Community Solving

Participation
